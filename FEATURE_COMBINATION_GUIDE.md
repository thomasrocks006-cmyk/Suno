# Feature Combination Guide

## Overview
The Suno app has three powerful lyric generation features that work together intelligently:

1. **Commercial Mode** 🟢 - "Less is More" approach for radio-ready songs
2. **Advanced Lyric Logic** 🔴 - Professional songwriting craft and metadata
3. **Central Metaphor** 🔵 - Thematic cohesion through unified imagery

## How Features Interact

### Priority Hierarchy
When multiple features are active, they follow this priority order:

```
1. Commercial Mode (Structure/Length Constraints)
   ↓
2. Advanced Lyric Logic (Quality/Craft)
   ↓
3. Central Metaphor (Thematic Unity)
```

---

## All 7 Combinations

### 1️⃣ **No Features Active** (Baseline)
**What You Get:**
- Standard song structure
- Basic metatags ([Verse], [Chorus])
- Natural lyric flow
- Minimal constraints

**Best For:** Quick experiments, testing ideas

**Example Output:**
```
[Verse]
I miss the way you used to smile
The way we'd talk for hours
Now everything feels empty
And I'm lost in these lonely towers

[Chorus]
Come back to me
Come back and stay
Don't let this love just fade away
```

**Strengths:** ✅ Fast, flexible, easy to understand
**Weaknesses:** ⚠️ Can be generic, lacks depth, no optimization

---

### 2️⃣ **Commercial Mode ONLY** 🟢
**What You Get:**
- **4-6 line verses** (strictly enforced)
- **Repetitive choruses** (2-4 core lines)
- Energy level marking (X/10)
- (M), (F), (M+F) vocal cues
- Concrete imagery encouraged
- Strategic humming/ad-libs

**Best For:** Radio singles, playlist-friendly tracks, Suno optimization

**Example Output:**
```
[Verse 1 – male lead, acoustic, 3/10 energy]
(M) I saw you at the corner store,
(M) Rain falling, just like before.
(M) You didn't see me standing there,
(M) Lost in the wet September air.

[Chorus – both, harmonies, 7/10 energy]
(M+F) We're ghosts in the rain,
(M+F) Washing away the pain.
(M+F) We're ghosts in the rain,
(M+F) Never to meet again.

[Bridge – call-and-response, 8/10 energy]
(M) Can you hear me?
(F) Through the storm.
(M) Can you feel me?
(F) Cold and worn.

[Outro – stripped, guitar + rain sounds, 2/10 energy]
(M+F) Ghosts in the rain...
Huuummm...
```

**Strengths:** ✅ High listenability, Suno-optimized, memorable hooks
**Weaknesses:** ⚠️ Can feel simplistic, limited narrative depth

---

### 3️⃣ **Advanced Lyric Logic ONLY** 🔴
**What You Get:**
- Detailed section headers with instruments and energy
- "Furniture Rule" - concrete imagery anchored to objects
- Sensory details in every verse (smell, sound, touch)
- Avoids AI clichés (Tapestry, Symphony, Realm, Unfold, Ignite)
- Slant rhymes (near rhymes) instead of perfect rhymes
- Prosody matching (plosives for anger, sibilants for sadness)
- "Gut Punch" lines in chorus/bridge
- **6-10 line verses** allowed (more literary)

**Best For:** Lyric-focused albums, storytelling, artistic depth

**Example Output:**
```
[Verse 1 – male baritone, fingerpicked guitar, upright bass, 4/10 energy]
(M) The coffee cup is moldy on the coaster where you left it,
(M) Your lipstick stain like rust on porcelain.
(M) I haven't touched the dishes, haven't opened up the curtains,
(M) The morning light just amplifies the mess I'm in.
(M) Your toothbrush leans against the sink like it's still waiting,
(M) The shower drain still smells like your shampoo.
(M) I'm talking to the static on the TV screen at midnight,
(M) Because it sounds a little bit like you.

[Chorus – full band enters, drums, piano swells, 7/10 energy]
(M) Every room's a graveyard of the things you didn't pack,
(M) Every corner holds a ghost I can't send back.
(M) I'm drowning in the details—your perfume in the air,
(M) You're gone but you're still everywhere. (Everywhere...)

[Bridge – stripped to piano only, whispered vocal, 5/10 energy]
(M - Whispered) I should've said I'm sorry when I had the chance,
(M - Whispered) Now I'm just rehearsing for an empty room romance.
```

**Strengths:** ✅ Emotionally deep, literary quality, vivid imagery, professional craft
**Weaknesses:** ⚠️ Can be too dense for Suno, lengthy verses may confuse AI, slower pacing

---

### 4️⃣ **Central Metaphor ONLY** 🔵
**What You Get:**
- ONE central object/concept (e.g., Ocean, Flame, Rust, Driver)
- ALL imagery stays within that metaphor's universe
- Chorus states the metaphor clearly
- Verses explore consequences/settings of metaphor
- Bridge flips or intensifies the metaphor
- No length restrictions

**Best For:** Concept songs, poetic expression, thematic albums

**Example Output (Metaphor: "The Ocean" = Separation):**
```
[Verse 1]
The tide came in and swept you out beyond my reach,
I stood there on the shore and watched you disappear.
The salt burns in my lungs, I'm screaming at the breach,
But waves just swallow every word I need you here.

[Chorus]
You are the ocean, pulling me apart,
Drowning every promise, sinking every start.
Blue and endless, cold and deep and wide,
You are the ocean, and I'm lost in the tide.

[Verse 2]
The current took your hand, I couldn't hold on tight,
The depths between us grow with every passing day.
I'm floating on the surface, barely in the light,
While you descend to places I could never stay.

[Bridge]
The storm came without warning,
And the shipwreck left me mourning,
In the wreckage I am clinging,
To the driftwood you stopped bringing.

[Final Chorus]
You are the ocean, pulling me apart...
```

**Strengths:** ✅ Thematic coherence, poetic depth, unified imagery, memorable concept
**Weaknesses:** ⚠️ Can be too abstract, may limit creative freedom, no Suno optimization

---

### 5️⃣ **Commercial + Advanced Lyric Logic** 🟢🔴
**What You Get:**
- **4-6 line verses** (Commercial constraint)
- WITH concrete imagery and sensory details (Advanced quality)
- Energy levels and vocal cues (both)
- Avoids AI clichés (Advanced)
- Repetitive choruses (Commercial)
- Slant rhymes, conversational phrasing (Advanced)

**Best For:** **RADIO-READY HITS WITH DEPTH** - This is the sweet spot for commercial success with artistic credibility

**Example Output:**
```
[Verse 1 – male lead, acoustic guitar, 3/10 energy]
(M) Your perfume's on the pillow, fading like your voice,
(M) I found your keys beneath the couch where we'd rejoice.
(M) The fridge still hums your favorite song in broken time,
(M) I'm drowning in the furniture of what was mine.

[Chorus – both, piano + strings rise, 7/10 energy]
(M+F) Paper frames, holding us in light,
(M+F) Silhouettes of what we were, bright.
(M+F) If I whisper to the glass, will you reply?
(M+F) Even when the years have passed, you're alive.

[Verse 2 – female lead, violin joins, 4/10 energy]
(F) I walk familiar streets at dusk, your echo in my pace,
(F) Streetlamps hum like distant songs of you I can't erase.
(F) Ticket stubs and folded notes in rows inside the drawer,
(F) Evidence of what we were, but nothing more.

[Bridge – call-and-response, stripped to piano + cello, 8/10 energy]
(M) I remember winter evenings—
(F) Your breath across the pane.
(M) Your laughter soft like falling snow—
(F) Your face inside the rain.

[Final Chorus – full band, soaring harmonies, 10/10 energy]
(M+F) Paper frames, holding us in light...

[Outro – piano + violin fade, 2/10 energy]
(M+F) Under these paper frames you'll live on...
```

**Strengths:** ✅✅✅ **OPTIMAL** - Commercial structure + emotional depth, Suno-optimized with literary quality
**Weaknesses:** ⚠️ Requires balance - can't let depth override brevity

### 6️⃣ **Commercial + Central Metaphor** 🟢🔵
**What You Get:**
- **4-6 line verses** (Commercial)
- ALL imagery from ONE metaphor universe (Metaphor)
- Repetitive choruses stating the metaphor (both)
- Energy levels and vocal cues (Commercial)
- Short bridge with metaphor flip (both)

**Best For:** **CATCHY CONCEPT SONGS** - Radio-friendly tracks with a unified theme (e.g., "You are the sun, I am the moon")

**Example Output (Metaphor: "The Flame" = Hope/Endurance):**
```
[Verse 1 – male lead, soft guitar, 3/10 energy]
(M) I've walked through shadows, I've carried the night,
(M) You held the matches, you gave me the light.
(M) The embers still whisper the shape of your name,
(M) Through ashes and silence, we carry the flame.

[Chorus – both, harmonies, 5/10 energy]
(M+F) Carry the flame, don't let it die,
(M+F) Through broken glass and starless sky.
(M+F) Every step, we find our way,
(M+F) Carry the flame into the day.

[Verse 2 – female lead, cello enters, 4/10 energy]
(F) I've held the sorrow, I've tasted the rain,
(F) You sang me mercy, you softened the pain.
(F) And even in darkness, the spark will remain,
(F) Through all of the breaking, we carry the flame.

[Bridge – call-and-response, 8/10 energy]
(M) I won't falter—
(F) I won't fall.
(M) Through the silence—
(F) I'll give it all.
(M+F) And if the night tries to hide our names,
We'll still burn on, we'll carry the flame.

[Final Chorus – full band, 10/10 energy]
(M+F) Carry the flame, don't let it die...

[Outro – guitar + violin, 2/10 energy]
(M) Through ashes and silence—
(F) Through sorrow and rain—
(M+F) We carry the flame...
Carry the flame.
```

**Strengths:** ✅ Catchy + thematic, easy to remember, unified concept, Suno-optimized
**Weaknesses:** ⚠️ Metaphor must be simple enough for short verses

### 7️⃣ **Advanced Lyric Logic + Central Metaphor** 🔴🔵
**What You Get:**
- **6-10 line verses** allowed (Advanced freedom)
- Concrete imagery from ONE metaphor universe (both)
- Detailed section headers (Advanced)
- Sensory anchors within metaphor (Advanced)
- "Gut punch" lines using metaphor (both)
- Avoids AI clichés (Advanced)

**Best For:** **LITERARY CONCEPT ALBUMS** - Deep, artistic work for lyric-focused audiences

**Example Output (Metaphor: "The Driver" = Loss of Control):**
```
[Verse 1 – male baritone, fingerpicked guitar, ambient slide, 4/10 energy]
(M) I'm asleep at the wheel, the road's a blur,
(M) The yellow lines keep bending like they're laughing at the turn.
(M) Your voice is in the rearview, fading in the distance,
(M) I grip the leather steering but I've lost all my resistance.
(M) The dashboard glows like warnings I refuse to understand,
(M) The brake lights up ahead spell out the words: "You can't."
(M) I'm swerving through the wreckage of the routes we used to take,
(M) And every exit missed becomes another choice I'll make too late.

[Chorus – drums enter, piano swells, 7/10 energy]
(M) I'm driving in the dark with my headlights off,
(M) Can't see the crash ahead but I can hear the scoff
(M) Of fate behind the wheel while I'm just a passenger,
(M) Watching life collide—it's a massacre. (A massacre...)

[Verse 2 – stripped to upright bass + brushed snare, 4/10 energy]
(M) The GPS keeps telling me to turn around and try again,
(M) But I'm too far gone down this road to find where I've been.
(M) Your seatbelt's still unbuckled on the passenger side seat,
(M) The airbag stains still smell like your defeat.
(M) I'm running on empty, fumes and broken dreams,
(M) The engine's choking out, unraveling at the seams.

[Bridge – emotional climax, controlled shouting, full band, 9/10 energy]
(M - Shouting) I should've pulled over! I should've hit the brakes!
(M - Shouting) Now I'm hydroplaning through my worst mistakes!
(M - Whispered) Can someone take the wheel... before it's too late?

[Final Chorus – intense, all instruments, 10/10 energy]
(M) I'm driving in the dark with my headlights off...
```

**Strengths:** ✅ Maximum artistic depth, literary quality, vivid and cohesive, emotionally powerful
**Weaknesses:** ⚠️ Too dense for Suno AI, may confuse generation, not radio-friendly, lengthy

## Testing Guide

To test each combination:

1. **Create a test song** with a simple concept (e.g., "Lost Love," "Hope," "Regret")
2. **Toggle features** one at a time and compare results
3. **Look for:**
   - Verse length (Commercial: 4-6 lines, Advanced: 6-10 lines, Metaphor: varies)
   - Concrete imagery (Advanced: "coffee cup moldy on coaster" vs. "I miss you")
   - Metaphor consistency (Metaphor: all ocean words vs. mixed imagery)
   - Energy levels (Commercial + Advanced: X/10 markers)
   - Vocal cues (Commercial + Advanced: (M), (F), (M+F))

4. **Analyze quality:**
   - Is it catchy and singable?
   - Does it have emotional depth?
   - Is the imagery vivid and cohesive?
   - Would Suno generate this well?

## Integration Success

The features now work as a **unified system** rather than independent layers:
- Commercial Mode sets the constraints
- Advanced Lyric Logic adds quality within those constraints
- Central Metaphor provides thematic unity across the structure

This ensures **every combination adds value** without conflicts! 🎵
